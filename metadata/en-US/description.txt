Vent first. Then send the version that actually wins.

RoastMate is the emotional-expression tool you wish you'd had after the last argument. Describe what happened in your own words, choose your tone — Composed, Sharp, Savage, Feral, or Vent — and the on-device AI gives you a response that helps you say what you actually mean. Feral and Vent create private drafts just for releasing the feeling — then a single tap rewrites them into sharp, safe-to-send replies that hold your ground without crossing any lines.

Pick from 20+ writing styles — Polite, Passive-Aggressive, Japanese Keigo, Literary, Stand-up, MBA Buzzword, Shakespearean, Savage, Corporate Passive Violence, Argument Killshot, and more — to match the register of who you're talking to.

Composed, Sharp, and Savage intensities run 100% on your device using Apple's Foundation Models framework. Vent and Feral private drafts route through our private cloud proxy (DeepSeek V3 via OpenRouter) so the rage actually lands — Apple's on-device model is too gentle for real venting. You can toggle Cloud AI off in Settings to keep everything local; vent output will be much gentler. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising IDs. Per-device rate-limited so abuse can't drain shared free quota.

RoastMate is a creative writing and emotional expression tool. It is not designed to target or harass any individual — names, threats, and slurs are automatically removed by a built-in safety filter.

PRIVACY

Calm / Sharp / Savage / Rewrite — fully on-device with Apple's Foundation Models. Vent / Feral private drafts route through a developer-owned Cloudflare Worker that forwards to DeepSeek V3 via OpenRouter, stateless and per-device rate-limited. You can disable cloud routing in Settings → AI & Privacy. Sign in with Apple is optional; when signed in, only an Apple-issued User ID is stored locally. No analytics, no advertising IDs, no fingerprinting.
