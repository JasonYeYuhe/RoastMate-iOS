Vent first. Then send the version that actually wins.

RoastMate is the emotional-expression tool you wish you'd had after the last argument. Describe what happened in your own words, choose your tone — Composed, Sharp, Savage, Feral, or Vent — and get a response that helps you say what you actually mean. Feral and Vent create private drafts just for releasing the feeling — then, where Apple's on-device model is available, a single tap rewrites them into sharp, safe-to-send replies that hold your ground without crossing any lines.

Pick from 24 writing styles — High EQ, Passive Aggressive, Japanese Keigo, Literary, Stand-up Bit, Shakespearean, Corporate Passive Violence, Argument Killshot, and more — to match the register of who you're talking to.

Composed, Sharp, Savage and the rewrite run 100% on your device, on Apple's Foundation Models where available. Vent and Feral use a cloud model, and only after you give permission: Apple's on-device model is too gentle for real venting, so the cloud lets them write real drafts on any device. Switch Cloud AI off in Settings → AI & Privacy and Vent and Feral show curated examples instead.

Without Apple's on-device model (iOS 18, macOS 14–15, or Apple Intelligence off or unsupported), Composed, Sharp and Savage (in every tool), the Argument Simulator and the one-tap rewrite show curated examples — always labeled, and never charged a credit.

RoastMate is a creative writing and emotional expression tool. It is not designed to target or harass any individual — the AI is told to replace real names with roles and never to use slurs or threats, and a built-in safety filter blocks threats and self-harm language.

PRIVACY

With your permission, what you type for Vent and Feral goes through a developer-owned Cloudflare Worker to third-party LLM providers; the Worker keeps none of your text and rate-limits each device. Sign in with Apple is optional; if you sign in, your Apple-issued User ID (plus name and email, if you share them) is stored only on your device. No tracking, no advertising IDs, no fingerprinting, no third-party SDKs. Anonymized usage counters are off by default; turned on, they stay on your device until you export them.
