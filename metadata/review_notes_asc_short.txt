Reviewer notes — RoastMate v1.6.0

1. WHERE GENERATION RUNS, AND WHAT A DEVICE WITHOUT APPLE INTELLIGENCE GETS.
Calm / Sharp / Savage and the "Make it sendable" rewrite run ON-DEVICE via
Apple's Foundation Models. The app also supports iOS 18 / macOS 14, where
Foundation Models does not exist. On such a device — or with Apple
Intelligence off, or while its assets are still downloading — those modes
return a small set of CURATED EXAMPLE responses instead of a model answer.
Every surface labels it ("Curated example — not generated from what you
typed. No credit used.") and NO credit is charged. This is intended.

Vent and Feral are different: Apple's on-device model refuses to produce
genuine vent output, so these two modes send the typed situation to our own
Cloudflare Worker, which forwards it to a third-party LLM provider (currently
Groq, OpenRouter as fallback). This happens ONLY after a separate, explicit
in-app consent (Guideline 5.1.2(i)) shown before any text leaves the device
— it names Vent and Feral — and is revocable anytime in Settings > AI &
Privacy. Our proxy is stateless; we do not retain text. Vent and Feral
therefore give real output on ANY device. The "roommate group" in the Echoes
tab is cloud-only for the same reason, behind the same consent.

2. POSITIONING.
A creative-writing / emotional-expression tool, not a harassment tool. No
field for a target person's name — only a SITUATION. Safe Mode on by default.
Vent and Feral produce PRIVATE drafts labelled "for yourself only", not
presented as messages to send. A user-initiated "Make it sendable" runs a
second pass that strips profanity, slurs, threats, sexual content and doxxing.

TEST PATH (no purchase or sign-in): a fresh install receives 10 free
starter credits. Roast Generator > Intensity "Vent" > type any situation >
Generate. The cloud consent sheet appears first; tap Allow. The card is
labelled "Vent draft - for yourself only" with a do-not-send banner and one
credit is used. "Make it sendable" rewrites it on-device at no charge (on a
device without Apple Intelligence it returns a labelled curated example).

3. WHAT'S NEW IN 1.6.0.
- Vent no longer requires Pro (it still uses credits and the consent above):
  on a device without Apple Intelligence it is a free user's only real output.
- The Explore tools (Reply Helper etc.) now show the same consent sheet
  before a Vent/Feral draft instead of silently staying on-device.
- The Pro screen no longer lists Vent, and says plainly when Savage can only
  show curated examples on the current device.
- Traditional Chinese: Taiwan/Hong Kong devices report "zh_TW"/"zh_HK" (no
  script tag), so they were told to answer in Simplified. Fixed.
- Some capabilities ship DISABLED behind remote flags.

4. iCLOUD SYNC IS ACTIVE. History, threads, saved situations, settings and
credit-ledger entries mirror via SwiftData + CloudKit into the PRIVATE
database of the Apple Account on the device (container
iCloud.yyh.roastmate.app) — the user's own storage. It never reaches our
servers and we cannot read it.

5. ACCOUNTS. Sign in with Apple is OPTIONAL and gates no feature; only the
Apple user ID and any shared name are stored locally in Keychain. No content
shared between users, no social features, no analytics or advertising SDKs,
no third-party SDKs.

6. PURCHASES (products unchanged from 1.3.1).
- yyh.roastmate.app.pro.monthly ($2.99/mo)
- yyh.roastmate.app.pro.yearly ($19.99/yr, 7-day trial)
- Consumable credit packs: .credits.10 / .70 / .160 / .380
Pro unlocks Savage, Feral and the Pro styles and removes the credit meter.
Pro is verified server-side: the app sends the StoreKit 2 transaction JWS to
our Worker, which validates Apple's signature and issues a short-lived token
for the higher cloud quota. The JWS carries no name, email or payment detail.

7. Share Extension and App Intents expose only Calm / Sharp — neither can
produce a private vent draft. 17+; first launch shows an age gate.
