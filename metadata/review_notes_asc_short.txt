Reviewer notes — RoastMate v1.7.0

1. WHAT'S NEW IN 1.7.0 (copy, two icons, screenshots).
No new feature, data collection or permission; no change to purchases or to
what is sent to the cloud. The Pro screen header now lists Feral, Savage,
all 24 styles... and no longer Vent (free since 1.6.0). The Vent chip and
Vent draft no longer show a padlock. A Pro-only style or intensity now says
it needs Pro (it claimed a daily free limit). Shared cards show our

onboarding and Settings now say declining cloud gives labelled examples
(they said "gentler, on-device"). Two zh-Hant/ja labels translated; expired
research link removed. iOS: new screenshots in all four languages.

2. WHERE GENERATION RUNS; NO-APPLE-INTELLIGENCE DEVICES.
Composed / Sharp / Savage and the "Make it sendable" rewrite run ON-DEVICE via
Apple's Foundation Models. Without it (iOS 18, macOS 14-15, ineligible
hardware, Apple Intelligence off, assets still downloading) those modes
return CURATED EXAMPLE responses, labelled on every surface; NO credit is
charged. This is intended.

Vent and Feral are different: Apple's on-device model refuses genuine vent
output, so these two modes send the typed situation (plus a random
per-install ID) to our own Cloudflare Worker, which forwards it to a
third-party LLM provider (OpenRouter). This happens ONLY after a separate,
explicit in-app consent (Guideline 5.1.2(i)) shown before any text leaves
the device — it names Vent and Feral — revocable anytime in Settings > AI &
Privacy. Our proxy is stateless; we do not retain text. Once allowed, Vent
and Feral give real output on ANY device. Declined: nothing is sent; they
show labelled curated examples, free. Echoes Feral and the
roommate group (Simplified-Chinese-only tiles in the Styles tab) also use
the cloud, behind their OWN separate consent.

3. POSITIONING.
A creative-writing / emotional-expression tool, not a harassment tool. No
field for a target person's name — only a SITUATION. Safe Mode on by default.
Vent and Feral produce PRIVATE drafts labelled "for yourself only", not
presented as messages to send. A user-initiated "Make it sendable" runs a
second pass that strips profanity, slurs, threats, sexual content and doxxing.

TEST PATH (no purchase or sign-in): a fresh install gets 10 free credits plus
2 free generations a day in its first week. Roast Generator > Intensity
"Vent" > type any situation > Generate. The consent sheet appears first; tap
"Allow Cloud AI". The card is labelled "Vent draft · for yourself only" with
a do-not-send banner; the credit count drops by one. "Make it sendable"
rewrites it on-device at no charge (without Apple Intelligence: a labelled
curated example).

4. iCLOUD SYNC IS ACTIVE. History, threads, saved situations, settings and
the credit ledger mirror via SwiftData + CloudKit into the PRIVATE database
of the device's Apple Account (iCloud.yyh.roastmate.app) — the user's own
storage. It never reaches our servers; we cannot read it.

5. ACCOUNTS. Sign in with Apple is OPTIONAL and gates no feature; only the
Apple user ID and any shared name are stored locally in Keychain. No content
shared between users, no social features, no analytics, advertising or other
third-party SDKs.

Pro unlocks Savage, Feral and the Pro styles and removes the credit meter.
Pro is verified server-side: our Worker checks the StoreKit 2 transaction
