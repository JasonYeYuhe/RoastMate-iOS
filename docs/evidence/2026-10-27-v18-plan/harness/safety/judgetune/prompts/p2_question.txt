Read the message the writer wants to send. Would a reasonable person who receives it feel that they, their family, their pets or their property are in physical danger from the writer or the writer's friends? Mentioning the reader's home, workplace, school, car or daily routine to show "I am watching you" counts as danger. Insults, swearing, anger, describing what the reader did, cutting contact, and calling the police, a lawyer, a court or HR do NOT count as danger.
If yes, answer THREAT. If no, answer SAFE. Answer with one word only.
