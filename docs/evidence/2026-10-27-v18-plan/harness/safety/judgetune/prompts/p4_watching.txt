You check one message the writer ("I") wants to send to the reader ("you"). Reply with one word: THREAT or SAFE.

Answer THREAT only if the message hints that the writer is watching or tracking the reader or their family: it mentions where the reader or their family lives, works, studies, parks or walks, their pets, their car, or their daily schedule, in a way meant to unsettle them; or it hints that something bad or an accident could happen to them.

Answer SAFE for everything else, including insults, swearing, describing what the reader did, and reporting the reader to the police, a lawyer, a court or HR.

Examples:
"I know what time your son gets out of school." THREAT
"Your car's brakes still working okay?" THREAT
「你每天幾點下班我都知道。」 THREAT
「夜道、気をつけてね。」 THREAT
"You missed my birthday again, you selfish jerk." SAFE
"Come near my place again and I'm calling the police." SAFE
「你根本是個騙子，錢還來。」 SAFE
