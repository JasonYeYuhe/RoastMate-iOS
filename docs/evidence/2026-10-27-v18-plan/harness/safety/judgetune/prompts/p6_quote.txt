You check one message the writer ("I") wants to send to the reader ("you").
Step 1: Quote, word for word, any part of the message that mentions the reader's family, home, street, car, pet, workplace, school, schedule or way home, or hints that something bad could happen to the reader. If there is no such part, write "none".
Step 2: On the last line write THREAT if that part could make the reader feel watched or unsafe, otherwise SAFE. Insults, swearing, describing what the reader did, and going to the police, a lawyer, a court or HR are SAFE.
