Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

  • On a device with Apple Intelligence, Calm, Sharp, Savage, the "Make it sendable" rewrite and the Argument Simulator run on your device using Apple's Foundation Models framework. That text never leaves your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. On a device without Apple Intelligence, these modes show labelled examples and send nothing.
  • Vent and Feral drafts use our cloud proxy only after you allow it. Then the situation text you type, the intensity, the style (its name and id), your UI locale, and an opaque per-install UUID go to our Cloudflare Worker, which builds a prompt and forwards it to a third-party LLM provider (OpenRouter). You can turn this off at any time in Settings → AI & Privacy → Vent / Feral via Cloud AI; Vent and Feral then show labelled examples, because Apple's on-device model does not write them.
  • In the Simplified Chinese app, the Roommate group works the same way, under its own permission (Settings → AI & Privacy → Roommate group on the cloud).
  • Our proxy does not store your text. It keeps short-lived counters to enforce daily limits (see 1a). Upstream LLM providers may retain requests temporarily under their own policies.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional. If you choose to sign in, your Apple User ID is stored in your device's Keychain.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, no device fingerprinting. The optional usage summary in Settings is off by default and leaves your device only if you share it yourself.

1. On-device generation (Calm / Sharp / Savage / Rewrite / Argument Simulator)

On a device with Apple Intelligence, the text you write for these modes is sent to Apple's Foundation Models framework, which runs the AI model on your device's CPU/GPU/Neural Engine. No part of that text is transmitted to RoastMate AI or to any third party. These paths work in Airplane Mode. On a device without Apple Intelligence, or with it turned off, these modes show examples written in advance and labelled as such; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Generated responses are stored on your device in a SwiftData store, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — mirrored to your own private iCloud database (see section 3). You can delete entries from History at any time.

1a. Cloud generation (Vent / Feral, Roommate group)

Only after you allow it — the app asks the first time you choose Vent or Feral — the app sends the following to a Cloudflare Worker we operate: the situation text you typed, the chosen intensity (vent or feral), the style's name and id, your UI locale, and an opaque per-install UUID. If you subscribe to Pro, the request also carries a session token from our Worker showing that the subscription is active (see section 4, Purchases). The Worker builds the prompt server-side and forwards it to a third-party LLM provider (currently OpenRouter, which passes it to a hosting provider for the selected model; we may change providers in the future).

In the Simplified Chinese app, the Roommate group (虚拟舍友群) sends the same fields under a separate permission, which it asks for first. Both of its tones are generated in the cloud; without that permission it does not run.

Our Worker does not retain a copy of your request text or the generated response. To enforce daily limits it keeps short-lived counters: one per install UUID; one per IP address, stored only as a keyed hash (the address itself is not stored); and, for Pro, one per subscription, stored as a hash of the App Store transaction id. It also remembers for a few minutes whether a subscription is active, under a hash of that id. These counters expire within about 48 hours. The Worker sends operational logs to Datadog: the outcome, status code, latency, intensity, locale, mode, the model that answered, and whether the request was on the Pro lane — never your text, your device UUID, your IP address, or any account identifier.

The upstream LLM providers each have their own privacy policies; in summary they do not retain customer prompts long-term but may retain them temporarily (typically up to 30 days) for reliability, abuse-prevention, or compliance purposes. If this is unacceptable to you, turn off Settings → AI & Privacy → Vent / Feral via Cloud AI (and, in the Simplified Chinese app, Roommate group on the cloud). Vent and Feral then show labelled examples instead, and the Roommate group does not run.

2. Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can use RoastMate AI fully without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Apple:

  • Apple provides us a stable, app-specific user identifier and, optionally, a name and email (real or relayed).
  • We store the user identifier in your device's Keychain. We do not transmit it off the device.
  • The name you share is displayed in the app's Account screen and may be synced to your other Apple devices via iCloud. It is never shared with us.
  • You can sign out at any time from Settings → Account → Sign Out. Your local data and iCloud-synced data remain.

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your history syncs across your devices through your own private iCloud database (CloudKit container iCloud.yyh.roastmate.app). To be explicit about what that covers, because the wording here used to be vaguer than it should have been: it includes your roast history and your private Vent and Feral drafts, along with threads, saved situations, settings, and credit-ledger entries.

This is your iCloud storage, not ours. The sync uses Apple's infrastructure; we do not operate, and cannot access, any server that holds this content, and none of it is ever sent to us. There is no in-app switch for it — it follows your device's iCloud state. Signing out of iCloud, or turning off iCloud Drive for RoastMate AI in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud, stops it.

4. App Store Connect "Privacy Nutrition Label"

The data types we collect, as defined by Apple's framework:

  • Other User Content — the situation text you type, when you use a cloud path you allowed (Vent / Feral, or the Roommate group). Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to user: No. Used for tracking: No.
  • Device ID — an opaque per-install UUID sent with cloud requests for rate limiting. Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to user: No. Used for tracking: No.
  • User ID — when you sign in with Apple. Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to user: Yes. Used for tracking: No.
  • Name — only if you choose to share it during Sign in with Apple. Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to user: Yes. Used for tracking: No.
  • Purchases — if you subscribe to Pro, the app sends the Apple-signed StoreKit transaction receipt to our Cloudflare Worker so it can confirm the subscription is genuine, issue the session token described in 1a, and grant the higher cloud quota. It identifies the transaction only; it carries no name, email, or payment details. Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to user: No. Used for tracking: No.

We do not collect: location, financial info, health & fitness, sensitive info, contacts, search history, browsing history, identifiers beyond those listed above, usage analytics, advertising IDs, or any content beyond the cloud requests you explicitly allow (which you can turn off).

5. Third parties

RoastMate AI integrates no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK. For the cloud paths (Vent / Feral and, in the Simplified Chinese app, the Roommate group), the request is proxied through Cloudflare (our Worker host) and forwarded to a third-party LLM inference provider (currently OpenRouter, which passes it to a hosting provider for the selected model). The Worker's operational logs, which contain no text and no identifiers (see 1a), go to Datadog. Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) is used automatically whenever you are signed in to iCloud, to sync your data to your own private iCloud database (see section 3). No other third parties are involved.

6. Children

RoastMate AI is rated 17+/18+ (IARC) for mature humor and sarcasm. It is not intended for children under 17.

7. Your rights

Our cloud proxy does not retain a copy of your request text, so there is no historical content stored at us to access, export, or delete; the rate-limit counters expire automatically within about 48 hours. In-app data can be deleted entry by entry in History or by uninstalling the app (for the iCloud copy, see section 3). To stop all cloud routing, turn off Settings → AI & Privacy → Vent / Feral via Cloud AI and, in the Simplified Chinese app, Roommate group on the cloud.

8. Changes
