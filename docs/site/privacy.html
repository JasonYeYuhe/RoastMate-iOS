Privacy Policy

RoastMate · Effective 2026-05-15

This policy describes how the RoastMate iOS, macOS, and watchOS app handles your data. By installing or using the app, you agree to the practices below.

TL;DR

RoastMate keeps almost all your data on your device. There is one optional exception: the Vent and Feral intensities route through a developer-owned cloud proxy by default, because Apple's on-device model is too gentle to produce real vent output. You can disable this in Settings to keep everything local.

  • Calm / Sharp / Savage / Make-it-sendable rewrite — 100% on-device via Apple's Foundation Models, always. Works in Airplane Mode.
  • Vent / Feral — by default the situation text you type, the intensity, the locale, and an opaque per-install UUID are sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which forwards to a third-party LLM provider for inference. Our proxy does not retain a copy of the text. Upstream providers may temporarily retain inputs per their own policies.
  • Your history, threads, and saved replies are never sent to us. They are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, mirrored through Apple's CloudKit into your own private iCloud database so your history follows you across your devices. We have no access to it.
  • No analytics SDKs, no advertising IDs, no device fingerprinting.
  • Toggle Cloud AI off in Settings → AI & Privacy to keep the entire app local.

Information we collect

Cloud Vent / Feral requests are the only inputs the app sends to servers we operate. Each request contains:

  • The text of your situation (the input you typed).
  • The intensity you chose (vent or feral).
  • The style name you chose.
  • Your UI locale (zh-Hans, ja, en, etc.).
  • An opaque per-install UUID generated on your device, used exclusively to enforce a daily rate-limit per device. It is not linked to your Apple ID, name, email, or any other identity.

What our proxy does with the request:

  1. Validates input length and intensity.
  2. Increments the per-device daily counter in Cloudflare KV (we keep only the counter, not the text).
  3. Builds the system prompt server-side and forwards your situation + locale to whichever upstream LLM provider we're currently using.
  4. Returns the generated text to your device. Our proxy does not retain a copy of either the request text or the response.

What the upstream providers do: Groq, OpenRouter, and any other third-party LLM provider we route to each have their own privacy policies that govern what they do with API requests. In summary: by default these providers do not retain customer prompts long-term, but they may retain them temporarily (typically up to 30 days) for reliability investigation, abuse prevention, or compliance purposes. Refer to their published policies for the authoritative terms. If this is unacceptable to you, turn Cloud AI off in Settings — the local-only path will still produce a (gentler) Vent draft using Apple's on-device model.

Other network traffic the app may initiate:

  1. App Store / StoreKit — when you open the paywall or make a subscription purchase, system frameworks talk to Apple. We do not see your purchase details directly; we only learn whether you have an active subscription via Apple's Transaction API.
  2. Sign in with Apple — if you choose to sign in, Apple's system sign-in flow talks to Apple. We store only the Apple-issued user identifier and any name/email you choose to share locally in Keychain, so Settings can show your account state.
  3. Crash reports — if you have iOS / macOS Diagnostics & Usage sharing enabled, Apple may send crash reports to us. These contain no personal information.

We do not use any AI service beyond the upstream providers described above for Vent / Feral. We do not embed analytics SDKs (Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Sentry, etc.). We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA, IDFV for tracking, AppsFlyer, etc.). We do not fingerprint your device.

Information stored locally on your device

The following is stored only on your device, inside the app's sandbox / App Group container:

  • Roast history. The situations you typed and the responses generated. Stored in SwiftData. You can delete individual entries or clear all sample data from Settings.
  • Settings. Your language preference, Safe Mode toggle, daily quota counter, and onboarding state.
  • Subscription state. Whether you have an active Pro subscription, derived from Apple's StoreKit framework.
  • Optional account state. If you use Sign in with Apple, the Apple-issued user identifier and any shared name/email are stored locally in Keychain.

On the same device, app-family targets can use the App Group group.yyh.roastmate.app as a local shared container where supported. Cross-device iCloud sync IS active: roast history (including private Vent and Feral drafts), threads, saved situations, settings and credit-ledger entries are mirrored via SwiftData + CloudKit into the private iCloud database of the Apple Account signed in on the device (container iCloud.yyh.roastmate.app). That is your own iCloud storage — it never reaches our servers and we cannot read it. It follows your device's iCloud state.

Children

RoastMate is rated 17+ for mature humor and sarcasm. It is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — including children.

Your rights

Our proxy does not retain a copy of Vent / Feral request text, so there is no historical content stored at us to access, export, correct, or delete. The per-device daily rate-limit counter in our KV store expires automatically within 48 hours of the day it covers. To remove all local data, uninstall the app or use Settings → Clear sample data and Settings → Manage subscription. To opt out of cloud routing entirely, toggle off Settings → AI & Privacy → Stronger Vent / Feral (Cloud AI); the app will then run all paths locally.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change the policy (for example, when changing which upstream LLM providers we route to, or adding new cloud features), we will update the "Effective" date and post a notice in the app on next launch. Any new cloud feature beyond the current Vent / Feral path will be opt-in and clearly described.
