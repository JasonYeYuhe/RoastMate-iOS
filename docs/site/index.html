Frustrated by a message you don't know how to reply to? Got hit with a passive-aggressive Slack and went blank? RoastMate turns that frustration into clever, safe, ready-to-send expression. Most modes — Calm, Sharp, Savage, and the "Make it sendable" rewrite — run on-device via Apple's Foundation Models framework. Vent and Feral private drafts route through our developer-owned cloud proxy for stronger output, which you can disable in Settings → AI & Privacy at any time.

Private by design

  • Calm / Sharp / Savage / Rewrite — 100% on-device via Apple's Foundation Models. Works fully offline.
  • Vent / Feral — opt-out cloud routing through a developer-owned Cloudflare Worker to a third-party LLM provider. Our proxy doesn't retain a copy of your text; upstream providers act under their own policies. Per-device daily rate limit.
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no fingerprinting
  • Sign in with Apple is optional — the app works fully signed-out
