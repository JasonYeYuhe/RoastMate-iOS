帮你骂 · On-device AI for witty, safe self-expression

Frustrated by a message you don't know how to reply to? Got hit with a passive-aggressive Slack and went blank? RoastMate turns that frustration into clever, safe, ready-to-send expression using on-device AI — Apple's Foundation Models framework, running entirely on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Watch.

Private by design

  • All AI generation runs on-device via Apple's Foundation Models
  • No third-party AI service — no OpenAI, no Anthropic, no Google
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no fingerprinting
  • Works fully offline (try Airplane Mode)
  • App Store privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected

Features

  • 20+ writing styles — High EQ, Passive Aggressive, Japanese Keigo, Shakespeare, MBA Buzzword, 文言文, 京片儿, 台剧腔, more
  • Reply Helper — paste a message, get suggested replies
  • Argument Simulator (Pro) — rehearse a tough conversation; AI plays the other side, multi-turn
  • Emotion Translator — turn raw honesty into the register you need
  • Social Roast — paste a post, get reaction replies ready to share
  • Apple Watch — quick roast on your wrist, hand off to iPhone
  • macOS menu bar — one-click roast from anywhere
  • iOS Share Extension — roast text from Mail, Messages, Safari
  • Siri & Shortcuts — via App Intents
  • 4 languages — English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語

Important note

RoastMate is a creative writing and emotional expression tool. It is not designed to target or harass any individual. The app automatically removes names, threats, and slurs from input and output through a built-in safety filter. Safe Mode is enabled by default.
