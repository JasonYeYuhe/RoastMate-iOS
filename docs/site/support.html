Frequently asked questions

Does RoastMate work offline?

Mostly yes. Calm, Sharp, Savage and the "Make it sendable" rewrite all run on-device via Apple's Foundation Models and work in Airplane Mode. The Vent and Feral intensities route to our cloud proxy by default and need a network connection — but you can switch them to local in Settings → AI & Privacy → Stronger Vent / Feral (Cloud AI), after which the entire app works offline (with gentler vent output).

Does RoastMate send my data to any AI company?

For Calm / Sharp / Savage / Rewrite: no — those run entirely on Apple's on-device Foundation Models. For Vent / Feral private drafts: by default the situation text you type is sent through a Cloudflare Worker that we own and forwarded to a third-party LLM provider for inference. Our proxy does not retain a copy of your text. Upstream providers act under their own policies. You can opt out in Settings to keep everything local. See the Privacy Policy for the full data-flow description.

I tapped a sample but want it gone from my history.

Settings → Clear sample data removes the bundled samples. Or swipe to delete any individual entry on the History screen.

I can't see the on-device AI working — what's happening?

RoastMate's on-device path requires iOS 26+, macOS 26+, or watchOS 26+ on hardware that supports Apple Intelligence. On unsupported devices the app falls back to a small set of curated responses instead of generating fresh ones. Vent / Feral (when Cloud AI is enabled) work on any supported device since they don't depend on Apple Intelligence.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device and find RoastMate Pro. Cancel at any time before the next renewal. You will keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period.

I'd like to request a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not RoastMate. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open Settings → Restore purchases. As long as you're signed in to the same Apple ID, your Pro access will reappear.

Apple Watch doesn't show my history.

Cross-device history sync is not part of the current 1.0 release. The watch app is designed for quick prompts and Handoff back to iPhone; full CloudKit sync is planned for a later update.

The AI suggested something I don't like / it's offensive.

The models are creative; outputs vary. Tap "Try again" for a fresh take. If you find a serious problem, please email support@colorarchive.me with the exact prompt — we'll tune the safety filter or the style prompt for the next release.
